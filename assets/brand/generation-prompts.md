# Image-generation prompts

The supplied Docked Shells proposal was used as the identity reference for both refinements.

## Shared specification

Use case: logo-brand.

Asset type: production master native app icon for Nauclio.

Primary symbol: preserve the unmistakable construction of three terminal/session tiles nested inside a three-spoke ship's helm or command wheel.

Refinement: make the wheel and three tiles optically balanced and symmetric; simplify tiny details; improve negative space; use a bold silhouette that remains legible at 32 px; preserve the centered hub and three-spoke geometry.

Style: premium native developer-tool icon, crisp vector-like geometry with restrained dimensional depth, not photorealistic, no texture.

Palette: Abyss #071426, Deep Current #0B1F3A, Cobalt #2563EB, Aegean Cyan #22D3EE, Seafoam #5EEAD4.

Constraints: preserve the selected concept; no words, letters, watermark, robot, brain, anchor, ship, or Kubernetes-like many-spoked wheel; exactly three session tiles and three structural spokes.

## Dark variant

Near-black navy squircle background filling the full canvas with no white outside border. The wheel uses restrained cobalt-to-cyan lighting; seafoam is a small accent. Strong contrast and a sophisticated dark appearance.

## Light variant

Warm mist-white squircle background filling the full canvas. The symbol uses deep navy structure, cobalt and cyan faces, and a subtle seafoam accent. Shadows are extremely light and clean, with accessible contrast and no pastel treatment.
